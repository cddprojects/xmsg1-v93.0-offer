Privacy Policy

Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

Curataskr protects your personal data in line with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This policy says what we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

1. Who we are

Curataskr is a registered Singapore company. “We”, “us”, and “our” mean Curataskr and its authorised staff.

For data-protection questions, write to hello@curataskr.com.

2. What personal data we collect

When you send an enquiry through this website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp or mobile number

We do not collect sensitive personal data through this website, including NRIC numbers, financial account details, or medical information.

3. Why we collect your data

We collect your data to:

  • Assess whether you suit the part-time work available
  • Contact you by WhatsApp or email about your enquiry
  • Onboard you as a Curataskr member if you are selected
  • Send relevant updates about opportunities — you can opt out at any time

We will not use your data for a purpose that is not listed here unless you consent.

4. How we use your data

Only Curataskr staff use your personal data. We use it to:

  • Review your profile and decide whether you are suitable
  • Contact you on WhatsApp within 24 hours if you are a suitable candidate
  • Arrange work and payment if you join as a member

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We may share it with:

  • Curataskr team members who handle recruitment and operations
  • Service providers who help us run our systems, such as cloud storage and communication tools, and who are bound by confidentiality

We disclose your data to anyone else only when the law or a Singapore authority requires it.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Unsuccessful applicants: up to 12 months from the enquiry, then securely deleted.
  • Active members: for the working relationship, and for up to 2 years after it ends, for compliance.

7. How we protect your data

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, or change. Those steps include access controls, secure communication channels, and confidentiality duties for staff.

8. Your rights under the PDPA

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Withdraw consent for us to use your data at any time — this may affect our ability to process your application
  • Ask us to delete your personal data, subject to any retention the law requires

To use any of these rights, email hello@curataskr.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. A browser may use session cookies for basic function. We do not use those to identify you.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. A material change will show a new date below. If you keep using the website after we post a change, you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your personal data:

  • Email: hello@curataskr.com
  • Company: Curataskr, registered in Singapore

You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) if you believe your data rights have been infringed.
