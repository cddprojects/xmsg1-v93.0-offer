Quick answers

Questions you’re already asking.

Is this a legitimate work-from-home side job?

Yes. FlexEarn SG is a registered Singapore company — you can check our UEN on ACRA BizFile. The work is from home. We’ve paid out over $2.1 million to more than 8,400 members. There is no upfront fee, no MLM, and no recruiting.

Can I do this part-time alongside my full-time job?

Yes. That’s how most members use it. Evenings, lunch hours, weekends. It fits around your job. It does not replace it.

How and when do I get paid?

Every Friday, by PayNow or bank transfer. The first payout is usually within 7 days of your first task. There is no minimum and no holding period.

Is there any registration fee?

No. Joining is free. If anyone asks you to pay to join FlexEarn, that is not us.
